1. On the **Projects** page, select **Create Project**.

![Create a project from the Projects page](/img/whats-on-your-agenda/create-demo-project/5.png)

2. Enter a project name, set **Project type** to **Demo**, and select **Create project**.

![Name the project, choose the Demo project type, and create it](/img/whats-on-your-agenda/create-demo-project/6.png)

3. In the new project, open **Project settings > General** and scroll to **Copy from...**.

![Find the Copy from section in the demo project's general settings](/img/whats-on-your-agenda/create-demo-project/7.png)

4. Open **Copy from** and choose **Willow (reference demo)**.

![Choose the Willow reference demo](/img/whats-on-your-agenda/create-demo-project/8.png)

5. Keep the content you want selected, then choose **Preview**. Review the documents, conversations, workflow runs, boards, and anything that will be left out.

![Preview the content selected for copying](/img/whats-on-your-agenda/create-demo-project/9.png)

6. Choose **Copy** and wait until the content copy reports **Copied**.

![Copy the selected content from the reference demo](/img/whats-on-your-agenda/create-demo-project/10.png)

7. Choose **Copy its context engine**. Keep the completed run that published the reference Context Engine selected, then choose **Copy the context engine**.

![Start copying the reference demo's Context Engine](/img/whats-on-your-agenda/create-demo-project/11.png)

8. Wait while Adri AI copies the Context Engine and its linked content.

![Monitor Context Engine copy progress](/img/whats-on-your-agenda/create-demo-project/12.png)

9. Confirm that the Context Engine copy reports **Copied**.

![Confirm that the Context Engine copy completed](/img/whats-on-your-agenda/create-demo-project/13.png)

10. Open **Context Engine** and confirm that the copied processes are available.

![Copied processes in the demo project's Context Engine](/img/whats-on-your-agenda/create-demo-project/14.png)

**Done when:** The content and Context Engine copy activities both report **Copied**, and the copied processes appear in your demo project.
